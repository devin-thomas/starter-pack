Lantern Street Tool Library lends hand tools, power tools and garden equipment to its members, so a neighbor can borrow a drill for a weekend project instead of buying one.

## Opening hours

- Tuesday and Thursday: 4 PM to 8 PM
- Saturday: 9 AM to 1 PM

We are closed on public holidays.

<!-- open-now -->

## Where to find us

The library is in the back room of the Lantern Street community center. Use the side door on Mill Lane.
