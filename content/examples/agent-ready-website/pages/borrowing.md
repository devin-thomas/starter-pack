## Membership

Membership costs **$12 a year**. Anyone who lives or works near Lantern Street can join at the front desk with a proof of address. Members under 18 need a parent or guardian to join with them.

## Loan limits

Members can borrow up to **three tools at a time**. A loan lasts **14 days**, except for high-demand tools, which have a **7-day** loan. The [tool catalog](/examples/agent-ready-website/tools) shows the loan length for each tool.

You can renew a loan once, for the same length again, if no other member is waiting for the tool.

## Late returns

There are no late fees. When a tool is more than 7 days overdue, your borrowing is paused until it comes back.

## Damaged tools

Tell the desk when you return a tool that is damaged. Normal wear is expected and costs nothing. If a tool is lost, the desk will talk with you about a fair replacement.
