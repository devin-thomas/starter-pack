## Why use this Rust guide?

Rust makes ownership and borrowing part of the source contract. This guide carries the established preference for explicit types, named states, deliberate failure handling, and clear resource owners into Rust's native language tools.

## Finished and approved

Rust 1.0.0 is the stable house guide. All 36 rules are accepted and ready for agents to read and apply; no further owner decisions or guide-level approval remain. RS-Q01 B permits narrowly contained first-party unsafe for necessary integration and demonstrated systems or performance requirements, under the evidence, ownership, and safety conditions in RS025–RS026.

## How to apply it

Use this guide as authoritative Rust guidance alongside the target repository's toolchain, public contracts, and existing decisions. Keep runtime, dependencies, targets, and application architecture specific to the project. Apply the accepted unsafe policy through its documented conditions and preserve any stricter local restriction until an authorized project change updates it. Future guide revisions do not make this version provisional.
