## Why use this Dart + Flutter guide?

Dart has strong static typing and Flutter has several valid state patterns; this guide applies explicit ownership and lifecycle rules without silently mandating a state-management package.

## Accepted house guide

Dart + Flutter 1.0.0 is the stable house guide. All 35 rules are finished and approved.

## How to apply it

Apply this guide within its stated language and ecosystem scope. Use the companion house guides where relevant, and keep project-specific versions, dependencies, product behavior, and deployment decisions in the target repository.
