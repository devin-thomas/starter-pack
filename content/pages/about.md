## Make something first

You do not need to master a long list of technologies before your first build. Start with one observable result. Then use that experience to build something meaningful and put it in the world.

## Made by Uppercut Labs

Starter Pack for Agentic Development brings my current recommendations and a practical agent companion into one free, accountless place.

## Your work belongs to you

There is no Starter Pack profile or progress dashboard. Your agent keeps progress in files you control and, by Phase 2, your own private GitHub repository. Keep passwords, access tokens, and other secrets out of those files.

This site uses aggregate analytics. Your setup progress stays with you and your agent.

## A living set of recommendations

Tools change. Each recommendation has a checked date and a reason, so you can see what it is for and ask your agent whether it still fits. This release covers Phases 1 and 2, with a preview of Phase 3.
