## Make something first

You do not need to master a long list of technologies before your first build. Start with one observable result. Then use that experience to build something meaningful and put it in the world.

## Made by Uppercut Labs

Starter Pack for Agentic Development brings my current recommendations and a practical agent companion into one free, accountless place.

## Your work belongs to you

There is no Starter Pack profile or progress dashboard. Your agent keeps progress in files you control. During Phase 2 setup, it saves that progress to your own private GitHub repository as soon as GitHub access is ready, before Quick Build begins. Keep passwords, access tokens, and other secrets out of those files.

This site uses aggregate analytics. Your setup progress stays with you and your agent.

## Feedback and project stories

You can [send feedback, request a feature, or share a project story](/contact), or write to [starter@devthomas.site](mailto:starter@devthomas.site).

The contact form sends only the fields you choose to submit. Your name and reply email are optional. Cloudflare processes the request, Make handles notification delivery and may retain execution history, and Devin receives the submission in Gmail. These submissions are separate from your private progress files; do not send passwords, access tokens, private conversations, or progress files.

Submissions are private by default. The optional project-story checkbox permits featuring your submitted story, project link, and name on Starter Pack; your email is never published. There is no automatic public posting. You keep ownership of your work. To ask about a submission, request deletion, or withdraw permission for future featuring, email starter@devthomas.site.

## A living set of recommendations

Tools change. Each recommendation has a checked date and a reason, so you can see what it is for and ask your agent whether it still fits. This release covers Phases 1 and 2, with a preview of Phase 3.
